### SCENE 4

**Scene Type:** TacticalBoard

**Narration:**
The second goal, six minutes later, started with a decoy rather than a shot. Mbappé collected the ball in the same central pocket, but instead of shooting again, laid it off and kept sprinting as if he still had it. That continued run dragged two Moroccan defenders a step deeper — still worried about him getting in behind — and Dembélé arrived late into the space they had just vacated. Whether Morocco's defenders were specifically drilled to fear that exact run again is impossible to say, but the same central spacing problem from the first half was still there in the sixty-sixth minute.
