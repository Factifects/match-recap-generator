### SCENE 5

**Scene Type:** Canvas

**Narration:** Trick four: subtracting awkward numbers. Round the number you're subtracting up to something clean, subtract that instead, then add back the difference you rounded by. Sixty-two minus twenty-nine: round to thirty, sixty-two minus thirty is thirty-two, add back the one you rounded by — thirty-three.

### SCENE 6

**Scene Type:** Canvas

**Narration:** Trick five, and here's the fun part — it's actually the same trick as multiplying by nine, just scaled up. Multiply by a hundred, then subtract the original number once. Thirty-four times ninety-nine: times a hundred is thirty-four hundred, subtract thirty-four, and you land on three thousand three hundred sixty-six. Same move works for nine, ninety-nine, nine hundred ninety-nine — anything sitting just short of a clean round number.
