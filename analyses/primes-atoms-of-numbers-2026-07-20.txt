### SCENE 6

**Scene Type:** Canvas

**Narration:** Back around 300 BC, Euclid proved something wild: there's no such thing as the largest prime — the list just never ends. His trick was beautifully simple: multiply every prime you know together, then add one. That new number can't be divided evenly by anything on your list — so either it's a brand new prime, or it's hiding one you missed. Either way? Your list was never actually complete.
