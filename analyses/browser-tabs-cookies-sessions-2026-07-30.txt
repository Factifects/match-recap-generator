**Scene Type:** Code

**Narration:** So what's actually written on that note? Think of a classic session like a coat-check ticket. The ticket itself is just a random number — completely meaningless on its own. Hand it to the counter, though, and they look up exactly whose coat that is. That's the whole trick: your real account info lives server-side, in a database, looked up by that string on every single request.
