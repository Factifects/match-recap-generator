### SCENE 6

**Scene Type:** Bar Chart

**Narration:** Every chip has a tiny processing delay — a fraction of a millisecond. For a slow, deep hum, that's nothing, barely three percent of one wave cycle. For a voice, oscillating far faster, that same tiny delay eats sixty percent of the cycle — by the time the anti-noise arrives, the real wave has already moved on.
