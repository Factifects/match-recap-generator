### SCENE 2

**Scene Type:** Line Chart

**Narration:** There are two ways interest can work. Simple interest pays you seven percent of your original deposit, every single year, forever. Compound interest pays you seven percent of whatever you've grown to — including every dollar of interest you've already earned. Watch year one: identical, both earn seventy dollars. From year two on, they never agree again.
