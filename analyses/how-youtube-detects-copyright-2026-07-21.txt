### SCENE 9

**Scene Type:** Grid

**Narration:** Once it matches, it's the rights holder — not YouTube — who picks what happens next. They can mute the audio, block the video in certain countries, or, the option chosen more than any other, just quietly take the ad revenue for themselves. That's called a claim, and it isn't the same thing as a strike — your channel isn't in danger, someone else is simply getting paid.

### SCENE 13
